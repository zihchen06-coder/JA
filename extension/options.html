Import education, work history & answers

Paste a JSON snippet shaped like {"education": [...], "experience": [...], "custom_answers": {...}} (any of the three keys is optional) — ask Claude to generate this from your resume or your own written answers — and it's added to the Education & Work and Answers tabs. Anything already there is matched up rather than duplicated (schools by school+degree, jobs by company+title, answers by keyword) and left as-is unless you tick Replace existing.

Let Claude answer what the matcher can't
After the normal fill, any field left unrecognised is sent to Claude along with your profile, and its answers are filled in. This is the one part of this tool that leaves your device: the field labels on the page and everything on these tabs (not your resume file, not your saved site logins) go to Anthropic's API under your own API key.

It will not answer self-identification, criminal-history, salary-history or consent questions, and it cannot tick a checkbox or a radio button — those stay yours, and that is enforced by the extension, not just asked of the model. Everything it does fill is outlined green for you to read before you submit.
Write a cover letter for each job
When a form has a cover-letter box, draft one for that specific role from the posting and your profile, instead of pasting the single saved letter from the Background tab into every application. Needs AI assist on.
Route my saved answers to oddly-worded questions
Self-identification, criminal-history and consent questions are only filled when the matcher recognises the wording. Turn this on and Claude also gets to work out which of your saved answers an unusual phrasing is asking for — including radio groups and tick boxes, which are otherwise left alone entirely.

It is still only ever choosing between answers you set on the Self-ID and Eligibility tabs. An answer that doesn't trace back to one of yours is dropped by the extension before it reaches the page, so nothing here can be inferred from your name or your resume. Questions you have left unset stay blank and flagged.
Show the side panel while filling
A panel on the right showing each step as it happens, every field and what was done to it (click one to jump to it on the page), what Claude was thinking, and a box to ask about any of it — including asking it to change a field. Turn this off and you get the small corner summary instead.
Watch what I type and remember it
After a fill, notice what you put into the fields it left blank, and use the same answer next time that question comes up. Costs nothing and needs no API key. Self-identification, criminal-history and consent questions are never picked up this way, nor is anything longer than a sentence or two, nor anything inside a work-history block. Everything remembered is listed on the Learned tab.
Fill automatically on known job sites
Fill each page of an application as it loads on Workday, iCIMS, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters and the rest, without clicking the icon. A six-page Workday application becomes one pass instead of six clicks. Still never submits anything. Note this spends API credit without a click, so it is off until you turn it on.
